# What This Project Is

Imagine you run a small business - you make honey, sew clothes, sell handmade soap - and you want a real online shop.
Not a clunky template, not a monthly subscription that bleeds you dry, but a proper, beautiful storefront that feels like *yours*.
That is exactly what this platform gives you.

## The idea in one breath

It is a **multi-tenant storefront platform**: one system that hosts many independent shops.
Each seller gets their own branded eshop at their own address, with their own colors, fonts, logo, products, and pages - and they manage all of it themselves through a clean admin panel.
No developer needed. No code. Ever.

## Why the "kind" eshop is special

Most platforms make you pay before you even know if you'll sell anything.
This one is **kind on purpose**:

- A seller can **start for free**.
  If they don't have a company yet, the platform handles the selling *for them* (commission mode) and only takes a small cut when they actually earn - 10% that drops to 5% as they grow.
  No sale, no cost.
- If they do have a company, they go **live instantly** and keep everything.
- Pricing is **one-time, never a subscription trap**.
  You are never punished for being small.

It lowers the barrier so far that *anyone* - a grandmother selling jams, a student selling prints - can have a professional shop today, and only pays when money is actually coming in.
That is what makes it genuinely generous, not just "free trial" generous.

## The storefront (what the customer sees)

Every shop is a full, modern online store: a styled homepage with hero banners, product cards, categories, blog, team page, FAQ, legal pages - all fully designed.
Customers can **browse and buy as a guest**, no forced account.
Shipping and payment are real and integrated (card payments, parcel pickup points, home delivery).
It loads fast and looks polished on every screen.

And the magic: a brand-new seller doesn't start with a blank page.
The system can look at their existing business website and **build their whole shop for them** - identity, colors, products, categories, about/contact text, legal documents - as a ready, editable starting point.
They open the admin and their store is already *there*, looking like them.

## The administration (what the seller runs)

This is the heart of it, and it is beautifully thought-out.

- **One panel runs everything**: products, variants, inventory, orders, returns, customers, pricing, reviews, reports, and a full content editor (navigation, pages, blog, team, FAQ).
- **A WYSIWYG homepage designer** lets sellers drag, reorder, recolor, and preview their *real* storefront live - 20 ready palettes and fonts - then hit publish.
- **Permissions are elegant and safe**: an owner sees all shops; an admin runs their assigned shops fully; and a shop can invite helpers with *exactly* the access they should have - "this person handles orders only," "this one manages products only."
  Everyone sees precisely their own sidebar, their own data, their own shop - nothing more, nothing less.
  No one can ever peek into someone else's store.
- **Inviting a teammate is effortless**: send an email, they set a password, and they land right inside the shop with the correct role already applied.

## What makes it brilliant

- **Kindness is built into the business model.** It wins when the seller wins.
- **Zero to professional shop in a day**, with content pre-built from the seller's own world.
- **Rock-solid, airtight access control** - every role sees exactly what it should, scoped per shop, with no leaks.
- **Legally grown-up** - real binding terms, privacy, and commission agreements in proper Czech and English, not placeholder text.
- **Everything the seller touches is theirs to control** - design, content, pricing, team - without ever needing a programmer.

In short: it takes the dream of "I want my own real online shop" and makes it **free to start, beautiful by default, safe to run, and fair to grow** - for people who would never otherwise get one.

That is why it deserves to be supported: it hands small sellers a professional storefront *and* a fair deal, at the same time.
